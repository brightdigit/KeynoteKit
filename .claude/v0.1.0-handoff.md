## Two premise corrections — do not re-derive

**1. There is no slide-count cap.** A 20-slide demo needs no new template.
`expandSlides` (`KeynoteArchiveSurgeon+Supply.swift:38-47`) loops
`cloneLastSlide` until the deck's count is met, and runs *before* the
`slideCountMismatch` guard (`KeynoteArchiveSurgeon.swift:78-88`) — so that guard
only fires when the deck has *fewer* slides than the template. `SupplyTests.swift`
already proves multi-slide expansion round-trips.

The real risk is cost, not capacity: `SlideCatalog.locate(recordIdentifier:)` is
a full nested linear scan, and `SlideCatalog(members:)` is rebuilt *inside the
`expandSlides` loop condition on every iteration* — roughly O(slides² ×
records). That is **#44**, and #63 is the spike that decides whether it gets
promoted into v0.1.0.

**2. Font family already works.** `TextBox.font(_:size:)` sets `fontName`,
threaded to `properties.fontName` in
`KeynoteArchiveSurgeon+CharacterStyle.swift:61-62`, and
`Sources/AcceptanceDeckCatalog/TextFormattingContent.swift:37` ships
`.font("HelveticaNeue", size: 48)` in a render-verified deck. Only *monospace
specifically* is unproven (#64) — the code-on-slide demo premise is not at risk.

## Syntax highlighting: why swift-syntax (#66)

Researched and empirically verified 2026-08-02. The three standard objections to
`apple/swift-syntax` are **outdated**:

- **Build cost** — 8.6s cold with prebuilts, which now default to enabled in
  SwiftPM 6.4 with no flag. From-source fallback is ~99s, not minutes.
- **Linux** — prebuilts exist for ubuntu noble/jammy, x86_64 + aarch64.
- **Toolchain pinning** — one `from: "603.0.0"` pin verified building *and
  running* on both Swift 6.3.2 and 6.4. Current stable 603.0.2 (2026-06-08).

Alternatives evaluated and rejected: **Splash** dormant (last release 2021, last
commit 2022) and mis-tokenizes string interpolation, raw strings, and regex
literals; **Highlightr**'s own README declares it unmaintained and it needs
JavaScriptCore; **SavannaKit** archived 2018; **Sourceful** effectively dead;
**swift-markdown/DocC** expose no reusable highlighting; **tree-sitter** needs
two C-interop dependencies and declares no Linux platforms.

Apple maintenance is the deciding factor — a hand-written lexer is a maintenance
burden we explicitly do not want. It lives in a **separate `KeynoteKitSyntax`
target** so core `KeynoteKit` keeps its single `swift-protobuf` dependency,
mirroring the `KeynoteKitScripting` split.

## Layout primitives (#65) — why this is cheap

`SlideDrawable` is a flat enum carrying absolute `x`/`y` per item
(`TextBox.swift:35-45`), and `SlideItemsBuilder` already flattens nested results
to `[SlideDrawable]`. Stacks are therefore a **pure compile-time layout pass**
resolving to the absolute positions the surgeon already writes — zero archive,
surgeon, or protobuf work. Lowest-risk lane in the release.

Intrinsic text measurement (#67) is split out precisely so it cannot block
#65, because measurement is coupled to #52 (below).

## Risks, most to least dangerous

1. **#52 is the riskiest item.** Text wraps at the layout master's ~816pt body
   width regardless of the authored frame — 300/600/1200pt probes produced
   identical column strides. The cheap geometry fix likely does not exist; the
   real fix is minting free `TSWP` shapes instead of cloning body placeholders,
   a text-path rewrite related to #2. **Timebox the probe. If it fails, demote
   to v0.1.1, author the demo at master body width, and document the
   limitation.** It must not gate the tag.
   Full width+height measurement (#67) depends on #52 landing; height-only
   measurement does not, which is why #67 has graduated fallbacks.
   **UPDATE:** #51's implementation independently confirmed this — the behavior
   is pre-existing and *not* caused by the shape-style fork, and column boxes
   likewise fill the master-derived height before the next column starts (hence
   one column box per slide in the acceptance decks). The risk stands as
   written; the diagnosis is now corroborated from two directions.
2. ~~**#74 (51d) is the largest PR**~~ — **RESOLVED, shipped in `e658a69`.** The
   breaking change did land: each bare `Text` in a `TextBox` builder is now its
   own paragraph, and `TextRunsContent.swift` was migrated accordingly.
3. **The manual render/export loop is the schedule risk**, not a technical one.
   Movie export prompts for TCC permission and may present an options sheet; no
   CI can catch regressions until #8. Budget two full human render cycles.
   Procedure to be written in #70. **This is now the top schedule risk**, and
   #51's own render pass is queued ahead of the demo's.
4. ~~**Rotation unit**~~ — **RESOLVED.** `TSD.GeometryArchive.angle` is
   **degrees, counterclockwise-positive**; `.rotationEffect` is SwiftUI
   clockwise-positive, so the surgeon writes `angle = −degrees` with
   `flags = 3`. Recorded in `research/findings/drawable_geometry.md`.
